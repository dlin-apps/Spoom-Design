Privacy Policy

Session Timer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Period.

Session Timer is a standalone Mac application made by Spoom Design LLC. We believe your data is yours, and a timer app has no business collecting it.

What the app stores:

  • Your timer settings (segment names, durations, warning thresholds, session name)
  • This data is stored locally on your Mac using UserDefaults
  • It never leaves your device

What the app does NOT do:

  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No network requests — the app works entirely offline
  • No accounts or sign-ins
  • No access to your files, contacts, camera, microphone, or location
  • No data shared with third parties

App Sandbox: Session Timer runs inside macOS App Sandbox with zero entitlements — no network access, no file access, no hardware access. It is as locked down as a Mac app can be.

Children's Privacy: The app does not collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes: If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page. Given that the app collects nothing, we don't anticipate any changes.
